Q1 How do I lock the screen and safely unlock it?
如何使用防误触锁屏与安全解锁？
After importing and framing your image, tap the orange "🔒 Lock" button in the top toolbar. The interface enters full-screen lock and stays awake with maximum brightness.
To unlock: Long-press the button at top-right for 2 seconds until the animated countdown ring completes to unlock and restore tools.
导入图片并调整到满意的位置后，点击顶部工具栏橙色的「🔒 锁屏」按钮，屏幕将立即全屏锁定并进入最高亮度。
如何解锁： 在屏幕右上角的悬浮解锁按钮上长按 2 秒（带红色进度圆环倒计时完成动画）即可解除锁定并恢复原有工具栏。
Q2 What paper is recommended for tracing?
垫纸描摹有什么推荐纸张与技巧？
Standard 70g or 80g A4 copy/printer paper, lightweight sketch paper, tracing paper, or vellum works great.
Because TraceLock boosts screen brightness to the maximum in lock mode, normal paper will clearly show lines underneath in typical room lighting.
推荐使用普通的 70g 或 80g A4 复印打印纸、薄素描纸、拷贝纸或硫酸纸。
由于应用在锁屏状态会自动将屏幕亮度调至最大，在暗光或普通室内光线环境下，普通白纸能非常清晰地透出屏幕上的素描线稿。
Q3 How to extract clear lines from busy or dark photos?
照片太黑、背景太花，怎么转成清晰线稿？
Use the "Sketch" or "Outline" filter in the bottom bar:
• "Sketch": Ideal for portraits, landscapes, and photos with rich shading, yielding pencil-like textures.
• "Outline": Best for anime, cartoons, and object contours, eliminating gradients and keeping clean outlines.
Tap the active filter to reveal the vertical purple slider on the right to fine-tune line weight and sensitivity in real time!
在底部滤镜栏中推荐使用「素描」或「轮廓」功能：
• 「素描」：适合人像、风景和带光影的复杂照片，保留丰富铅笔线条；
• 「轮廓」：适合动漫人物、简笔画及物体边界，能消灭杂色只留下纯黑外框。
点击选中的滤镜后，右侧会弹出紫色上下滑杆，上下滑动即可实时调整线条粗细与深浅！
Q4 Will my drawing state be preserved if I switch apps?
切换到其他应用再回来，之前的图片还在吗？
Yes! TraceLock features full session state persistence. Even if you switch to other apps or the OS clears the background process, reopening TraceLock restores your image, zoom/pan transform, and filter settings seamlessly.
在的！临摹板具备全状态持久化保护。即使切换到相机、其他应用，或者后台被系统回收，重新打开应用会自动无缝复原上次选择的图片、位置缩放大小与滤镜参数，无需重新选图。
Q5 Does TraceLock require internet access or in-app purchases?
应用需要联网吗？会扣费吗？
Importing, filtering, and tracing happen on-device and never upload your photos. An App Store connection is needed to load the localized price, buy the lifetime unlock, or restore a purchase. Every feature is free for 3 days; afterward, an optional one-time lifetime purchase is available (US base price: $3.99; the App Store displays your local price). There is no subscription and no advertising.
图片导入、滤镜和临摹均在本机完成，不需要联网，也不会上传照片。首次加载本地化价格、购买永久版或恢复购买时，需要连接 Apple App Store。全部功能可免费试用 3 天；试用结束后，可一次买断永久解锁（美国区基准价格为 3.99 美元，实际价格以 App Store 当地显示为准）。没有订阅、没有广告。
