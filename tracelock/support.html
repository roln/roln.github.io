Support & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

技术支持与常见问题
Official Support & User Guide for TraceLock: Tracing Lightbox (iPad & iPhone)
Q1 How do I lock the screen and safely unlock it?
如何使用防误触锁屏与安全解锁？
After importing and framing your image, tap the orange "Lock" button in the top toolbar. The interface enters full-screen lock and stays awake at maximum brightness.
To unlock: Long-press the floating button at top-right for 2 seconds until the animated countdown ring completes to unlock and restore tools.
导入图片并调整到满意的位置后，点击顶部工具栏橙色的「🔒 锁屏」按钮，屏幕将立即全屏锁定并进入最高亮度。长按右上角悬浮解锁按钮 2 秒即可安全解锁。
Q2 What paper is recommended for tracing?
垫纸描摹有什么推荐纸张与技巧？
Standard 70g or 80g A4 copy/printer paper, lightweight sketch paper, tracing paper, or vellum works great. Because TraceLock boosts screen brightness to the maximum in lock mode, normal paper clearly shows lines underneath in typical room lighting.
推荐使用普通的 70g 或 80g A4 复印打印纸、薄素描纸、拷贝纸或硫酸纸。锁屏状态会自动将屏幕亮度调至最大，普通室内光线下即可清晰透出线稿。
Q3 How to extract clear lines from busy or dark photos?
照片太黑、背景太花，怎么转成清晰线稿？
Use the "Sketch" or "Outline" filter in the bottom bar:
• "Sketch": Ideal for portraits, landscapes, and photos with rich shading.
• "Outline": Best for anime, cartoons, and object contours, eliminating gradients and keeping clean outlines.
Tap the active filter to reveal the vertical slider on the right to fine-tune line weight and sensitivity in real time.
在底部滤镜栏中使用「素描」或「轮廓」功能，并点击选中的滤镜调出右侧上下滑杆实时调节线条粗细与深浅。
Q4 Will my drawing state be preserved if I switch apps?
切换到其他应用再回来，之前的图片还在吗？
Yes. TraceLock features full session state persistence. Even if you switch to other apps or iOS/iPadOS clears the background process, reopening TraceLock restores your image, zoom/pan/rotation transform, and filter settings seamlessly.
在的。应用会自动在本地持久化保存图片、缩放/平移/旋转构图和滤镜参数，切出后回来可无缝继续。
Q5 Does TraceLock require internet access or have hidden charges?
应用需要联网吗？收费机制是怎样的？
Importing, filtering, and tracing happen entirely on your device with no network connection required and zero photo uploads.
• Free Trial: New users enjoy a full-featured 5-day free trial with no ads and no automatic subscriptions.
• Free Forever Core: After the trial, basic photo import, composition adjustments, and line-art conversion remain free forever.
• Lifetime Purchase: Touch-proof screen lock, A4 sheet export/print, and high-res export can be unlocked with an optional one-time Lifetime Purchase—no recurring subscriptions.
所有图片处理均在设备本地离线完成。新用户享有 5 天全功能免费试用（无广告、无自动续费）；基础选图、构图与线稿转换永久免费；锁屏透写、A4 打印/PDF 导出等进阶功能可通过一次性买断永久版解锁。
Q6 How do I generate A4 printable coloring sheets and showcase my artwork?
如何生成 A4 打印练习纸，以及进行临摹打卡？
• A4 Printable Sheets: Tap "A4 Print" in the gallery to generate standard A4 sheets with single or 2×2 / 3×3 grid layouts, synchronized zoom/pan, and AirPrint or PDF export.
• Artwork Showcase: Use the camera alignment tool to photograph your finished paper drawing side-by-side with the digital sketch—processed strictly on-device.
在作品库点击「A4 练习纸」即可排版打印或导出 PDF；临摹完成后可用本地相机对齐底稿生成打卡对比卡片。
Q7 How do I turn an iPad or iPhone into a tracing lightbox without the screen moving?
如何把 iPad / iPhone 当作透写台且防止手掌或纸张滑动画布？
Import your reference image into TraceLock, zoom and rotate it to match your paper, and tap Lock. TraceLock intercepts palm resting and paper sliding gestures across the entire screen, keeps the display awake at full brightness, and only unlocks when you hold the unlock ring for 2 seconds.
导入图片并调整好比例后点击「锁屏」，TraceLock 会拦截手掌和纸张误触并保持常亮高亮度，必须长按解锁按钮 2 秒才会退出锁定。
Q8 Can I use TraceLock for tattoo stencils, calligraphy, anime line art, or kids' drawing?
能用于纹身转印稿、英文书法、动漫线稿或儿童描红吗？
Yes. Creators use TraceLock across multiple workflows: horizontal mirror flip and 90° rotation for tattoo stencil prep, the Outline filter for clean anime and manga line art, A4 grid layouts for calligraphy and bullet journal practice, and simple line extraction for kids' tracing sheets. See our Use-Case Guides for step-by-step tips.
可以。支持水平镜像与旋转（适合纹身转印底稿）、轮廓提取（适合动漫线稿与儿童描红）、以及 A4 多图网格导出（适合书法与手帐练习）。
Q9 How does TraceLock compare to a physical LED light pad or AR camera tracing apps?
和实体 LED 拷贝台或 AR 相机临摹 App 相比有什么区别？
Unlike a physical LED light pad, TraceLock doesn't require printing a reference sheet first—you can zoom, crop, mirror, and convert any photo into clean line art directly on your iPad or iPhone. Unlike AR camera tracing apps that need a phone stand and careful lighting, TraceLock lets you lay paper flat on the screen and draw comfortably.
相比实体透写台，不需要先打印底稿即可直接缩放、镜像和转线稿；相比 AR 投影类 App，不需要支架和特定环境光，直接把纸铺在屏幕上就能画。
Q10 Does TraceLock require a subscription, account signup, or cloud upload?
需要注册账号、订阅扣费或上传照片到云端吗？
No. TraceLock requires no account signup, shows no ads, processes every photo 100% locally on your device, and offers an optional one-time Lifetime Purchase instead of a recurring subscription.
都不需要。无需账号、无广告、所有照片仅在本地处理，且采用一次买断制，没有订阅。
