Tracing Lightbox Use-Case Guides

Practical workflows for turning your iPad or iPhone into a reliable light table with TraceLock—all processed locally on-device with no account or subscription.

Core Workflow

1. Turn an iPad or iPhone into a Touch-Locked Tracing Lightbox

Resting your palm or sliding paper over a tablet normally triggers accidental zoom or swipe gestures. TraceLock eliminates that friction in five steps:

  1. Import your photo: Open TraceLock and pick any reference image from Photos.
  2. Pick a line filter (optional): Switch to Sketch, Outline, Comic, Black & White, or High Contrast so edges stand out through paper.
  3. Frame to your paper size: Pinch to zoom, drag to align, and rotate in 90° steps.
  4. Tap Lock: Screen brightness rises to maximum, auto-sleep is disabled, and canvas touch gestures are swallowed.
  5. Place paper and trace: Use 70g–80g copy paper, tracing paper, or vellum. Hold the top-right unlock ring for 2 seconds when you're done.
Anime & Portraits

2. Convert Photos to Clean Line Art for Sketching

Tracing directly over dark, shaded photos makes it hard to see where major shapes begin and end. Use TraceLock's on-device filters before locking the screen:

  • Portraits & still life: Choose Sketch (Color Dodge) to preserve gentle pencil-like shading transitions.
  • Anime, manga & objects: Choose Outline (Difference of Gaussians) to strip busy backgrounds down to crisp black lines on pure white.
  • Fine-tune thickness: Tap the active filter to reveal the vertical slider and adjust contour weight until faint eyes, hair, or folds appear cleanly through your paper.
Tattoo Stencils

3. Prep Tattoo Stencil Reference Sheets

When preparing flash designs or custom stencil transfers on thermal or tracing paper, alignment and orientation matter:

  • Horizontal mirror flip: Flip the design horizontally in one tap so traced transfer lines match final placement orientation.
  • High-contrast cleanup: Use Outline or High Contrast to isolate clean stencil lines from shaded reference art.
  • Lock or export A4: Either trace directly on an iPad in Lock mode, or export an A4 PDF / AirPrint sheet at exact scale.
Calligraphy & Journals

4. Practice Calligraphy, Hand Lettering & Bullet Journal Layouts

Instead of ruling faint pencil guidelines by hand on every page, keep a digital template locked underneath your stationery:

  • Import a calligraphy slant sheet, brush-lettering exemplar, or weekly bullet-journal grid.
  • Zoom to match your notebook's exact physical dimensions and tap Lock.
  • Place thin marker paper or journal pages over the screen—your hand can rest naturally while you letter.
Kids & Printable Practice

5. Kids' Drawing & Printable A4 Coloring Sheets

Need quick tracing or coloring sheets without hunting through ad-heavy websites?

  • Convert family photos, pets, or favorite characters into clean outlines on-device.
  • Open A4 Print to arrange a single sheet or a 2×2 / 3×3 grid with synchronized zoom and pan.
  • Send straight to an AirPrint printer or share a crisp A4 PDF—no internet upload required.
