Privacy Policy

隐私政策
Effective Date / 生效日期: November 4, 2026 · Version 1.1

Perler & Fuse Beads Studio ("the App") is developed as an offline-first creative utility for bead craft enthusiasts. We respect your privacy and follow a strict zero-data-collection philosophy.

Perler & Fuse Beads Studio（以下简称“本应用”）是一款致力于为拼豆手工爱好者提供极致对齐体验的创作工具。我们严格恪守零数据收集与离线优先原则，切实保护您的个人隐私与创作数据安全。

1. Zero Data Collection & 100% Offline Processing

1. 零数据收集与 100% 本地设备端离线运算
  • No Cloud Uploads: All image pixelation, palette reduction, and AI background removal occur entirely on your local device hardware using Apple Vision and CoreGraphics. None of your photos, camera captures, or bead designs are ever transmitted to any external server.
  • No Account Required: You can use all core features without registering, signing in, or submitting phone numbers, emails, or personal profiles.
  • No Third-Party Trackers: We do not integrate third-party ad networks, telemetry trackers, or profiling SDKs.
  • 绝无云端上传： 所有图像像素化转换、色盘量化及 AI 去背景运算全部在您的本地设备上离线完成。您的照片与图纸绝不上传至任何外部服务器。
  • 无需账号体系： 您无需注册、登录，亦无需绑定手机号或邮箱即可完整使用本应用。
  • 无三方广告与追踪： 应用内不包含任何第三方广告 SDK 或用户画像追踪组件。

2. Device Permissions Used

2. 涉及的系统权限说明
  • Photo Library (Read): Used strictly when you voluntarily choose to import an image from your photo album to generate a bead pattern.
  • Photo Library (Write / Add Only): Used strictly when you save exported pattern diagrams or A4 print sheets directly to your camera roll.
  • 相册只读权限： 仅在您主动从相册选取图片转为拼豆图纸时调用。
  • 相册写入权限： 仅在您选择将高清拼豆图纸或 A4 打印单导出保存至手机相册时调用。

3. iCloud Key-Value Sync (Optional)

3. iCloud 键值同步说明（可选）

To provide a smooth experience across your iPhone and iPad, the App stores a minimal launch timestamp in Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage (KVS) if enabled on your device. This only ensures your 5-day free trial status remains consistent across devices. No personal identifiers or pattern data are stored. You can disable this anytime via the App's Settings.

为保证您在 iPhone 和 iPad 多设备间的试用体验连贯，应用在您开启 iCloud 的前提下，会将首次启动时间戳同步至 Apple 官方的 iCloud 键值存储（仅用于试用天数计算，不包含任何个人身份信息）。您可以在应用设置中随时开关此功能。

4. In-App Purchase & Financial Information

4. 应用内购买与财务信息

Transactions for Lifetime VIP are handled exclusively by Apple App Store StoreKit. We have no access to your credit card, bank details, billing address, or payment credentials.

所有 Lifetime VIP 永久会员的购买与扣款均由 Apple 官方 StoreKit 结算完成。开发者完全无法获知或存储您的银行卡、支付密码或账单信息。
