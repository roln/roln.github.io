Workflow Guides & Best Practices

Everything you need to know about 1:1 physical pegboard calibration, AI background removal, and multi-board fuse bead projects.

1. Calibrating 2.6mm Mini vs. 5.0mm Midi Pegboards

Different fuse bead brands use two industry-standard peg spacings:

  • 2.60 mm (Mini Beads): Used by Artkal C-series, Mard 2.6mm, and Hama Mini. A standard 52×52 pegboard measures ~13.5 × 13.5 cm and fits comfortably on any iPad display.
  • 5.00 mm (Midi Standard Beads): Used by Perler, Hama Midi, and Artkal S-series. A 29×29 / 28×28 board measures ~14.0 × 14.0 cm.
  • Zero-Drift Alignment: Tap 2.6 or 5.0 at the bottom of the Lightbox to lock 1:1 physical scale based on your device’s hardware PPI.

2. Clean Photo-to-Pattern Conversion with AI Background Removal

Converting raw photos directly often turns walls, shadows, and furniture into dozens of unwanted bead colors.

  • Toggle AI Remove Background in Step 2 to isolate characters, pets, or portraits cleanly on-device.
  • Enable CIELAB Delta-E color matching and Merge Rare Colors to consolidate 1–2 bead outliers into their nearest neighbor shade.
  • Use Clean Noise in the Step 3 pixel editor to remove isolated stray pixels with one tap.

3. Assembling Multi-Board Wall Art (X×Y Boards)

Switch to Multi-Board mode in Step 2 when crafting large portraits or landscapes. Zooming automatically calculates how many interlocking 28×28 or 52×52 boards are needed (up to 6×6). Inside the Lightbox, tap any board tile in the board switcher to center and lock that exact section under your physical board.

4. Fast, Mistake-Free Beading with Solo Color & Touch Lock

Activate Lock Screen before resting your pegboard and wrists on the glass. Tap any color pill in the bottom tray to turn on Solo Color Focus—only pegs of that color stay lit while the rest dim by 90%, cutting assembly time and preventing missed beads.
