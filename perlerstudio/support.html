Perler & Fuse Beads Studio - Support & FAQ

Welcome to Perler & Fuse Beads Studio support. We are here to help you enjoy physical fuse bead crafting with precise on-screen lightboxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I align the real pegboard with my screen?

A: When entering the Lightbox workspace, tap "2.6mm" (for mini beads) or "5.0mm" (for midi beads) at the bottom. The app calibrates pixels to the exact physical millimeter scale of your screen. Place your transparent pegboard directly over the screen, and the pins will line up with the bead dots.

Q: How do I unlock the screen when touch-armor lock is active?

A: When locked, touch gestures are blocked so your hands and plates don't move the canvas. To unlock, simply press and hold the lock indicator in the upper-right corner for 1.5 seconds.

Q: What is the Solo Color Focus feature?

A: In the workspace, tap any bead color in the bottom swatch bar. Only that color will shine brightly while all other colors are dimmed by 90%, preventing mistakes and eye fatigue.
