Frequently Asked Questions

Does the 1:1 size really match physical 2.6mm and 5.0mm pegboards?

Yes. The engine queries your exact iPad or iPhone model identifier to obtain its native hardware PPI and converts millimeters to exact screen points, keeping every peg aligned from corner to corner.

What happens after the 5-day free trial ends?

You keep full access to your saved single-board patterns, 9 starter templates, blank canvas editor, and 1:1 physical lightbox with touch lock. Only multi-board stitching and Solo Color focus require the optional one-time Lifetime VIP upgrade.

Can I import images directly from Safari, Photos, or Files?

Yes. You can import from the system Photos picker, share images via the iOS Share/Open-In sheet, or open via the perlerstudio:// URL scheme.
