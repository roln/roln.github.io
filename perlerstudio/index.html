What it does

True 1:1 lightbox

Calibrates to the exact physical pitch of 2.6 mm mini beads or 5.0 mm midi beads, so the screen lines up with your real pegboards.

Square boards only

Work in single square boards, or let multi-board mode auto-calculate how many square boards are needed to cover the whole image.

Solo color focus

Tap a color code to highlight just that color and dim the rest — ideal for placing beads one color at a time.

Anti-mistouch lock

Lock the screen so your hands and real boards never shift the canvas. Long-press 1.5 seconds to exit.

Shopping list (BOM)

Every project generates a color-code list with exact bead counts, so you know what to buy before you start.

Fully offline

No account, no login, no server. Every photo is processed entirely on your device.

Getting started

  • Tap + and pick a photo.
  • Zoom, pan, and crop freely — the image is never stretched.
  • Pick your board size (28 / 52 / 64 pegs) and single- or multi-board mode.
  • Tap Generate to enter the lightbox workspace.
  • Lock the screen, place your real board on top, and start placing beads.
