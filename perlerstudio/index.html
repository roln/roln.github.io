Core Features

True 1:1 Physical Lightbox

Calibrates directly to the exact millimeter pitch of 2.6 mm (Mini) or 5.0 mm (Midi) beads. Place your transparent acrylic board on the screen to craft with zero eye fatigue.

AI Remove Background

One-tap on-device vision segmentation isolates characters, pets, and anime figures, preventing messy backgrounds from creating dozens of unwanted bead colors.

Standard & Multi-Board Grids

Supports single square boards (28, 52, or 64 pegs) or X×Y multi-board seamless stitch calculations for larger wall-art pieces.

Solo Color Highlighting

Tap any color swatch to glow just that bead shade at 100% while dimming the rest by 90% — ideal for assembling complex projects color by color.

Anti-Mistouch Lock

Rest your hand or place pegboards directly on the screen without canvas shifting. Long-press 1.5 seconds to safely unlock.

Shopping List (BOM) & Export

Auto-tabulates required bead counts mapped to popular palette codes (Artkal, Perler, Hama, Mard). Export HD images or share/print A4 sheets.

Quick 4-Step Creation

  • 1. Crop: Pick any photo, zoom, pan, and optionally enable AI Remove Background.
  • 2. Setup: Preview instant bead simulation, pick board size (28/52/64 or multi-board), and select color count.
  • 3. Editor: Fine-tune individual beads with pencil, fill bucket, and color picker tools.
  • 4. Export / Lightbox: Share or print A4 sheets, or place your board on the screen for 1:1 crafting.
