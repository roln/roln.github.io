VideoFit — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

1. Overview

VideoFit ("the App") is an on-device utility designed to compress videos to exact file-size budgets. We respect your privacy and operate under a strict zero-data-collection philosophy.

2. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • Videos Never Leave Your Device: All video decoding, downsampling, and re-encoding is executed 100% locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's native VideoToolbox framework.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no third-party tracking SDKs, analytics packages, or advertising frameworks integrated into the App.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use the App.

3. Photos & Files Access

When you select a video from Photos or Files, the App accesses only the specific video you deliberately selected through the system-provided photo picker. VideoFit does not scan, index, or upload your photo library.

4. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are handled directly by Apple through StoreKit 2. We do not process, receive, or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses. Purchases sync across your devices via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@matehub.net.
