Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why did VideoFit produce a video slightly smaller than my target?
A: VideoFit employs conservative convergence margins to ensure your file never exceeds the target budget. It is always better to be a fraction of a megabyte under budget than to be rejected by the messaging server.
Q: What video formats can I compress?
A: VideoFit supports all formats natively decodable by iOS, including MOV, MP4, M4V, H.264, and HEVC/H.265. HDR videos (HLG/PQ) are gracefully tone-mapped to standard SDR for universal recipient compatibility.
Q: Does VideoFit upload my videos to a server?
A: No. All compression runs 100% on your device using Apple Silicon hardware encoders. VideoFit works entirely offline in Airplane Mode.
Q: How do I restore my Pro purchase on another device?
A: Tap the Settings gear icon in the top right corner, then tap "Restore Purchases". StoreKit will verify your Apple ID license immediately.
